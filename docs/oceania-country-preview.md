# Australia and New Zealand low-value consumer imports

The two examples cover sales by a GST-registered overseas merchant who arranges delivery to a private resident in the destination country. The reviewed products are ordinary, standard-rated exercise books (482020), ballpoint pens (960810) and pencils in a rigid sheath (960910). Use one verified product line per consignment in this pilot. The line can contain multiple units.

| Destination | Transport | Verified aggregate goods value | Checkout GST on goods, seller-billed freight and insurance | At import |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Australia | International post | Up to and including AUD 1,000 | 10% | Customs duty and import GST of AUD 0 |
| New Zealand | Air freight, sea freight or international post | Up to and including NZD 1,000 | 15% | Customs duty and import GST of NZD 0. A goods levy can be passed on separately. |

The sample JP → AU shipment has AUD 300 of exercise books and AUD 50 postage. Its seller GST is AUD 35. The US → NZ shipment has a NZD 20 pen and NZD 10 shipping. Its seller GST is NZD 4.50. Both are synthetic examples. The amounts do not estimate brokerage, total landed cost or importer clearance.

## Preparing evidence

Supply a dated invoice or order reference and confirm in the evidence object that the nonresident merchant arranges delivery, the buyer is a private resident, all figures are GST-exclusive and the goods qualify as ordinary non-excisable stationery. `saleDate` is the date the price is agreed with the consumer. The supplied item price must be the agreed price, and all seller-billed freight and insurance must be supplied separately in the same currency. `taxEvidenceConfirmed` represents the reviewed seller GST record. Keep originals outside the preview and send only an opaque evidence reference.

Confirm the customs value and aggregation independently. The preview accepts the verified aggregate customs value only if it matches the full goods subtotal. In New Zealand this includes the check for other orders from the same supplier arriving on the same day for the same importer. When that check identifies additional orders, consolidate or refer the shipment for review. Prices requiring conversion, split or adjusted valuations, exemptions, mixed product lines and marketplace or business purchases require a separate assessment. Both the sale and proposed import date must fall within this pilot's review window of 24 November through 11 December 2026. Those dates describe this source review window.

## New Zealand levies

Under the schedule effective 1 April 2026, the combined Customs and MPI low-value freight levy is NZD 2.21 (air) or NZD 2.09 (sea), excluding GST. For an Inward Cargo Report it applies per low-value write-off at the house bill level. New Zealand Customs describes freight forwarders as the usual payers. UPU mail has its own levy based on the gross weight of incoming mail. The preview leaves the buyer's charge unresolved because the freight or postal operator's invoice determines any pass-through.
